        Major Global Payment Systems

        🌍

        SWIFT (Global Wire Transfer)

        Society for Worldwide Interbank Financial Telecommunication — used by 11,000+ banks in 200+ countries.

        • Standard for international bank-to-bank transfers
        • Requires: SWIFT/BIC code + IBAN/account number
        • Fee: $15-50 per transfer
        • Speed: 1-5 business days
        • Most reliable for tuition payments
        Essential200+ CountriesBank-to-Bank
        💚

        Wise (TransferWise)

        Best exchange rates with transparent fees. Available in 80+ countries, 50+ currencies.

        • Mid-market exchange rate (no markup)
        • Fee: 0.5-2% depending on corridor
        • Multi-currency account available
        • Wise debit card for spending abroad
        • Fastest growing fintech transfer service
        Best Rates80+ CountriesDebit Card
        🅿️

        PayPal / Xoom

        World's largest online payment platform. Xoom for international remittances.

        • PayPal: 400M+ users, 200+ countries
        • Xoom: fast transfers to bank/cash pickup
        • Student accounts with lower fees
        • Buyer/seller protection for online purchases
        • Not accepted everywhere in China
        400M+ UsersLimited in China
        🟠

        Mastercard / Visa

        Global card networks accepted in 210+ countries and territories.

        • Visa: 3.9B+ cards worldwide
        • Mastercard: 2.8B+ cards worldwide
        • Contactless payments (NFC/tap)
        • Foreign transaction fee: 1-3%
        • Travel insurance & purchase protection
        • Link to mobile wallets (Apple/Google Pay)
        210+ CountriesContactlessFX Fee 1-3%
